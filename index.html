怎么用

  • 按顺序读一个阶段：目标 → 核心概念 → 对照表 → 动画 → 实验 → 自测。
  • 动画可以点「播放」自动走，也可以用 ← → 逐步看，space 暂停。
  • 自测全对，那一块地就会变绿。分数只存在这台浏览器里，不上传。

How to use

  • Read a phase in order: goals → core concepts → comparison table → diagram → lab → quiz.
  • Diagrams play on their own, or step with ← →; space pauses.
  • A perfect quiz turns that plot green. Scores stay in this browser only; nothing is uploaded.

About

创始人的自述，原文为英文。

Learning Garden is my personal space for learning, experimenting, and understanding how things work. Inspired by the Garden of Eden and rooted in my Christian faith, it reflects a desire to understand God's creation with humility and care. I study underlying principles, test what I learn, and use that understanding to create and serve.

Deep Roots: understanding principles and mechanisms. Fresh Ideas: independent thought and creative use of knowledge.

Deep Roots：理解原理与机制。Fresh Ideas：独立思考，把理解用于创造。
