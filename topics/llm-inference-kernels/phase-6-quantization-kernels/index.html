量化把权重（有时连激活）存成更少的位：FP8 是 1 字节，int4 和 FP4 是半字节，再加上每一小组一个缩放因子。解码时 GEMM 受带宽限制（Phase 0），读的字节少了，时间才会少；但前提是有一个内核在寄存器里边读边解量化，或者直接让 tensor core 吃低精度的数。没有这样的内核，量化只省显存：SGLang v0.5.22 里强制用 --quantization awq 时，走的就是先把整张权重矩阵解成 FP16 写回显存、再调 torch.matmul 的路，字节反而更多。这台 Apple M5 上实测，16384 × 16384 的 GEMV：FP16 4205 µs，边读边解的 int4 1095 µs（快 3.84 倍），先解再算的 int4 9589 µs（只有 FP16 的 0.44 倍），三者的有效带宽都在 127–128 GB/s，约为 Apple 标称 153 GB/s 的 84%。哪些跑过、哪些没跑过：Lab B 的 Metal 程序 2026-11-01 在这台 Apple M5 上实际运行，数字原样贴出；Lab A 在 L4 上比较 FP8 与 int4 检查点的解码速度、跑一个 CuTe DSL 的 GEMM，未运行，是 2026-11-01 根据 SGLang v0.5.22 的源码和 CUTLASS、DeepGEMM 的文档整理的；NVFP4 与 MXFP4 的原生内核只在 Blackwell 上有，只作描述。

Quantization stores the weights, and sometimes the activations, in fewer bits: FP8 is one byte, int4 and FP4 half a byte, plus one scale per small group. Decode GEMMs are bandwidth-bound (Phase 0), so time drops only when fewer bytes are read; and that needs a kernel that dequantizes in registers as it reads, or tensor cores that take the low-precision numbers directly. Without such a kernel, quantization saves only memory: in SGLang v0.5.22, forcing --quantization awq takes the path that first dequantizes the whole weight matrix to FP16 in memory and then calls torch.matmul, which moves more bytes, not fewer. Measured on this Apple M5 for a 16384 × 16384 GEMV: FP16 takes 4205 µs, int4 dequantized on the fly 1095 µs (3.84 times as fast), and int4 dequantized first 9589 µs (0.44 times FP16), all three at an effective 127–128 GB/s, about 84% of Apple’s quoted 153 GB/s. What was run and what was not: the Metal program of Lab B was actually run on this Apple M5 on 2026-11-01 and its numbers are pasted as they came; Lab A, which compares the decode speed of an FP8 and an int4 checkpoint on an L4 and runs a CuTe DSL GEMM, is not run, assembled on 2026-11-01 from the SGLang v0.5.22 source and the CUTLASS and DeepGEMM documentation; the native NVFP4 and MXFP4 kernels exist only on Blackwell and are only described.

核心概念

Core concepts

解码每一步都要把全部权重从显存读一遍，时间几乎就是权重的字节数除以带宽，所以把每个权重从 2 字节压到 1 字节或半字节，看上去能直接快 2 到 4 倍。可是 4 位或 8 位的数单独表示不了权重的范围；而且如果内核先把压缩的权重解回 FP16 写进显存再去算，读写的字节反而比不压缩还多。量化要做到两件事：用缩放因子补回范围，并且让 GEMM 内核实际读到的字节真的变少。

Every decode step reads all the weights from memory once, and its time is almost the weight bytes divided by bandwidth, so shrinking each weight from 2 bytes to 1 or half a byte looks like a direct 2 to 4 times speedup. But a 4-bit or 8-bit number alone cannot cover the range of the weights, and if the kernel first expands the compressed weights back to FP16 in memory before computing, it moves more bytes than with no compression at all. Quantization has to achieve two things: recover the range with scales, and make the GEMM kernel actually read fewer bytes.

概念 1 的缩放粒度回答前一件；概念 2 的配套内核回答后一件，概念 3 的 Marlin 和概念 4 的块缩放 FP8 GEMM 是它的两种做法。

The scale granularity of concept 1 answers the first; the matching kernel of concept 2 answers the second, and Marlin in concept 3 and the block-scaled FP8 GEMM in concept 4 are its two forms.

1. 数字格式与缩放的粒度

1. Number formats and the granularity of scales

低精度格式只有很少的位，单独一个数表示不了权重的整个范围，所以每种方案都是「低精度的数 × 一个缩放因子」，区别在于每个缩放因子管多少个数。下面的最大值是从编码推出来的；FP8 的两个与 FP8 论文的表 1 一致：

Low-precision formats have so few bits that one number alone cannot cover the range of the weights, so every scheme is “a low-precision number times a scale”, and they differ in how many numbers each scale covers. The largest values below are derived from the encodings; the two FP8 ones match Table 1 of the FP8 paper:

  • FP8 E4M3 与 E5M2（Micikevicius 等，arXiv:2209.05433，读的是 v2）：都是 1 字节，4 位指数加 3 位尾数，或 5 位指数加 2 位尾数。摘要说 E5M2 按 IEEE 754 的惯例表示特殊值，E4M3 不表示无穷大、只留一个尾数位型表示 NaN，以此扩大范围。由此推出 E4M3 的最大值是 1.75 × 28 = 448，E5M2 是 1.75 × 215 = 57344（按偏置 7 和 15 计算，与 arXiv:2209.05433 的表 1 相同）。E4M3 精度高、范围窄，E5M2 反之；DeepSeek-V3 技术报告说他们在所有张量上都用 E4M3，因为细粒度的缩放已经补上了范围。
  • FP8 E4M3 and E5M2 (Micikevicius et al., arXiv:2209.05433, read at v2): both one byte, with a 4-bit exponent and 3-bit mantissa or a 5-bit exponent and 2-bit mantissa. The abstract says E5M2 follows IEEE 754 conventions for special values, while E4M3 extends its range by representing no infinities and keeping a single mantissa bit pattern for NaN. From this, the largest E4M3 value is 1.75 × 28 = 448 and the largest E5M2 value 1.75 × 215 = 57344 (computed with biases 7 and 15, and the same as Table 1 of arXiv:2209.05433). E4M3 has more precision and less range, E5M2 the reverse; the DeepSeek-V3 technical report says it uses E4M3 on all tensors, because fine-grained scaling already makes up the range.
  • int8 与 int4：整数加缩放（对称），或整数减零点再乘缩放（非对称）。int4 一个字节放两个，AWQ 的检查点把 8 个 int4 打包进一个 32 位整数（SGLang 的 awq_dequantize 的假实现把列数乘 8，hardware_backend/gpu/quantization/awq_kernels.py 第 59–62 行）。
  • int8 and int4: an integer times a scale (symmetric), or an integer minus a zero point, times a scale (asymmetric). int4 packs two to a byte, and AWQ checkpoints pack 8 int4 values into one 32-bit integer (the fake implementation of SGLang’s awq_dequantize multiplies the column count by 8, hardware_backend/gpu/quantization/awq_kernels.py, lines 59–62).
  • NVFP4 与 MXFP4：元素都是 4 位的 E2M1（1 位符号、2 位指数、1 位尾数，能表示 0、0.5、1、1.5、2、3、4、6 及其负数，推断：按偏置 1 计算），区别在缩放。MXFP4 是 OCP Microscaling 格式之一（Rouhani 等，arXiv:2310.10537，v3，摘要说 MX 把每块一个缩放因子与窄的浮点或整数元素结合），每 32 个元素一个 UE8M0（只有指数的 8 位）缩放；SGLang 的注释写作「packed e2m1 + ue8m0 g32」（quantization/__init__.py 第 93–94 行）。NVFP4 每 16 个元素一个 E4M3 缩放，外加整个张量一个 FP32 缩放：SGLang 的 ModelOptFp4LinearMethod 文档字符串列出 weight（NVFP4）、weight_scale（FP8-E4M3）、weight_scale_2（FP32 标量）（modelopt_quant.py 第 1747–1759 行）。PTX ISA 的块缩放 MMA 表与此一致：.kind::mxf4nvf4 的 E2M1 元素可配 UE8M0 缩放或 UE4M3 缩放（2026-11-01 读取）。
  • NVFP4 and MXFP4: both use 4-bit E2M1 elements (1 sign, 2 exponent and 1 mantissa bit, holding 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4 and 6 and their negatives; an inference, computed with bias 1), and differ in their scales. MXFP4 is one of the OCP Microscaling formats (Rouhani et al., arXiv:2310.10537, v3, whose abstract says MX combines a per-block scale with narrow floating-point or integer elements), with one UE8M0 (exponent-only, 8-bit) scale per 32 elements; SGLang’s comment reads “packed e2m1 + ue8m0 g32” (quantization/__init__.py, lines 93–94). NVFP4 has one E4M3 scale per 16 elements plus one FP32 scale for the whole tensor: the docstring of SGLang’s ModelOptFp4LinearMethod lists weight (NVFP4), weight_scale (FP8-E4M3) and weight_scale_2 (an FP32 scalar) (modelopt_quant.py, lines 1747–1759). The block-scaled MMA table of the PTX ISA agrees: E2M1 elements under .kind::mxf4nvf4 take either UE8M0 or UE4M3 scales (read 2026-11-01).

缩放的粒度从粗到细：整个张量一个（per-tensor）、每个输出通道一个（per-channel）、每个 token 一个（per-token，只用于激活）、沿 K 每 g 个一组（per-group，g 常取 128，int4 的 AWQ 和 GPTQ 用它）、每个二维块一个（per-block，DeepSeek-V3 的权重是 128 × 128）。组越小，离群值影响的范围越小，但缩放因子本身也要读：int4 每 128 个权重配 2 字节的 FP16 缩放和 1 字节的零点，就是每个权重多 3/128 字节，Lab B 的 3.8 倍而不是 4 倍正是这一点（概念 2）。

Scale granularity, from coarse to fine: one per tensor, one per output channel, one per token (activations only), one per group of g along K (per-group, g often 128, as int4 AWQ and GPTQ use), and one per two-dimensional block (per-block; DeepSeek-V3’s weights use 128 × 128). The smaller the group, the less an outlier spoils, but the scales themselves must also be read: int4 with a 2-byte FP16 scale and a 1-byte zero point per 128 weights adds 3/128 of a byte per weight, which is exactly why Lab B gets 3.8 times and not 4 (concept 2).

In SGLang v0.5.22’s registry, BASE_QUANTIZATION_METHODS (python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/__init__.py, lines 62–90, fetched with curl on 2026-11-01), the methods that matter here are: FP8 ones, fp8, mxfp8, w8a8_fp8, modelopt / modelopt_fp8 and w4afp8 (int4 weights with FP8 activations); int4 ones, awq, awq_marlin, gptq_marlin, moe_wna16 and auto-round, plus gguf and bitsandbytes; FP4 ones, modelopt_fp4, nvfp4_online, petit_nvfp4 (ROCm), quark_mxfp4 and mxfp_w4a8 (NPU), and mxfp4, registered only on CPU, CUDA, gfx95 and XPU (lines 97–102). 2. 只省内存不省时间：为什么要有配套的 GEMM 内核

2. Memory saved, time not: why a matching GEMM kernel is needed

解码一个 token，一层线性层 y = W x 要把 W 读一遍，每个权重只做一次乘加。时间约等于读到的字节数除以带宽。量化省时间的唯一来源，就是让内核实际读到的字节变少。有三种做法，只有后两种省时间：

Decoding one token, a linear layer y = W x reads W once and does one multiply-add per weight. The time is roughly the bytes read divided by the bandwidth. The only way quantization saves time is by making the kernel actually read fewer bytes. There are three ways to run a quantized layer, and only the last two save time:

  1. 先解后算。一个内核把整张 int4 矩阵解成 FP16 写回显存，再用普通的 FP16 GEMM 读回来。读 0.5 字节、写 2 字节、再读 2 字节，每个权重 4.5 字节，比直接存 FP16 的 2 字节还多。SGLang v0.5.22 的 AWQLinearKernel.apply 就是这样：out = awq_dequantize(qweight, scales, qzeros)，然后 out = torch.matmul(reshaped_x, out)（python/sglang/srt/hardware_backend/gpu/quantization/awq_kernels.py 第 97–98 行）。Lab B 的 int4_unfused 行把它量了出来：0.44 倍。
  2. Dequantize first, then multiply. One kernel turns the whole int4 matrix into FP16 in memory, and an ordinary FP16 GEMM reads it back. That is 0.5 bytes read, 2 written and 2 read again, 4.5 bytes per weight, more than the 2 bytes of plain FP16. SGLang v0.5.22’s AWQLinearKernel.apply does exactly this: out = awq_dequantize(qweight, scales, qzeros), then out = torch.matmul(reshaped_x, out) (python/sglang/srt/hardware_backend/gpu/quantization/awq_kernels.py, lines 97–98). The int4_unfused row of Lab B measures it: 0.44 times.
  3. 边读边解（仅权重，W4A16 / W8A16）。GEMM 内核从显存读 int4，在寄存器里移位、掩码、减零点、乘缩放，变成 FP16 喂给 FMA 或 tensor core，解出来的值从不写回。显存流量就是 0.5 字节加缩放。这是 Marlin（Frantar 等，arXiv:2408.11743，v1）的做法；摘要说 4 位权重时，批大小到 16–32 都能接近最大的 4 倍加速，到 64–128 时加速逐渐变小但仍然可观。Lab B 的 int4 行：3.84 倍。
  4. Dequantize as you read (weight-only, W4A16 / W8A16). The GEMM kernel reads int4 from memory, shifts, masks, subtracts the zero point and multiplies by the scale in registers, and feeds FP16 to the FMAs or tensor cores; the dequantized values are never written back. Memory traffic is 0.5 bytes plus scales. This is what Marlin does (Frantar et al., arXiv:2408.11743, v1); its abstract says that with 4-bit weights, batch sizes up to 16–32 get close to the maximum 4 times speedup, and larger batches of 64–128 a gradually smaller but still significant one. The int4 row of Lab B: 3.84 times.
  5. 低精度直接进 tensor core（W8A8 / W4A4）。激活也量化，tensor core 直接算 FP8 × FP8 或 FP4 × FP4，缩放在累加时乘上（概念 4）。解码时它省的同样是字节；预填充受算力限制时，它还能用上 tensor core 更高的低精度吞吐。NVIDIA 的 L4 页面列出 FP16 tensor core 242 TFLOPS、FP8 485 TFLOPS，两者都标注为带稀疏的数字（2026-11-01 读取）。
  6. Low precision straight into the tensor cores (W8A8 / W4A4). The activations are quantized too, the tensor cores compute FP8 × FP8 or FP4 × FP4 directly, and the scales are applied during accumulation (concept 4). In decode it saves bytes in the same way; in prefill, where the GEMM is compute-bound, it also uses the tensor cores’ higher low-precision throughput. NVIDIA’s L4 page lists 242 TFLOPS for FP16 tensor cores and 485 TFLOPS for FP8, both marked as figures with sparsity (read 2026-11-01).

所以同一个检查点在不同的 GPU 上省不省时间，取决于那张卡上有没有匹配的内核。SGLang v0.5.22 里三个例子：AWQ 检查点在 sm_80 以上默认被改走 awq_marlin（awq.py 第 333–354 行的 override_quantization_method），只有用户显式写 --quantization awq 才强制走先解后算，日志会提示改用 awq_marlin；FP8 检查点在 sm_80 到 sm_88 上（A100 这一类没有 FP8 tensor core 的卡）自动改走 Marlin 的 FP8 仅权重内核（fp8.py 第 506–512 行，判断在 fp8_utils.py 第 2540–2546 行，80 <= sm < 89）；NVFP4 检查点在 SM80 到 SM90 上走 Marlin 的 W4A16 后备，原生的 FP4 GEMM 要 SM100 以上，否则报错「ModelOpt NVFP4 native dense GEMM backends require SM100+」（modelopt_quant.py 第 1907–1911 行）。

So whether one checkpoint saves time on a given GPU depends on whether that card has a matching kernel. Three examples in SGLang v0.5.22: an AWQ checkpoint on sm_80 and above is moved onto awq_marlin by default (override_quantization_method in awq.py, lines 333–354), and only an explicit --quantization awq forces the dequantize-first path, with a log message suggesting awq_marlin; an FP8 checkpoint on sm_80 to sm_88 (A100-class cards without FP8 tensor cores) is moved onto Marlin’s FP8 weight-only kernel (fp8.py, lines 506–512, with the test in fp8_utils.py, lines 2540–2546, 80 <= sm < 89); and an NVFP4 checkpoint on SM80 to SM90 takes Marlin’s W4A16 fallback, since the native FP4 GEMM needs SM100 or later and otherwise raises “ModelOpt NVFP4 native dense GEMM backends require SM100+” (modelopt_quant.py, lines 1907–1911).

3. int4 仅权重：AWQ、GPTQ 与 Marlin 内核

3. int4 weight-only: AWQ, GPTQ and the Marlin kernel

AWQ 和 GPTQ 是两种离线把权重压到 4 位的算法，内核只关心它们产出的格式：

AWQ and GPTQ are two offline algorithms for squeezing weights into 4 bits; the kernel cares only about the format they produce:

  • GPTQ（Frantar 等，arXiv:2210.17323，v2）：摘要说它是基于近似二阶信息的一次性权重量化，能在约 4 个 GPU 小时内把 1750 亿参数的模型压到每个权重 3 或 4 位，精度损失可以忽略。
  • GPTQ (Frantar et al., arXiv:2210.17323, v2): the abstract describes a one-shot weight quantization based on approximate second-order information that brings a 175-billion-parameter model to 3 or 4 bits per weight in about four GPU hours with negligible accuracy loss.
  • AWQ（Lin 等，arXiv:2306.00978，v6）：摘要说并非所有权重都同样重要，保护约 1% 的显著权重就能大幅减小量化误差；显著的通道要看激活的分布而不是权重本身，并用一个等价变换放大这些通道来保护它们，缩放由离线收集的激活统计决定。
  • AWQ (Lin et al., arXiv:2306.00978, v6): the abstract says not all weights are equally important, and protecting about 1% of salient weights greatly reduces quantization error; salient channels are found from the activation distribution, not the weights, and are protected by an equivalent transformation that scales them up, with the scales set from activation statistics collected offline.

为什么要重排：边读边解的内核希望一个线程一次读 16 字节就拿到它马上要用的 32 个权重，并且解出来的 FP16 正好排成 tensor core 指令要的寄存器布局，不必再在线程之间搬数据。检查点里的打包顺序是为写检查点的工具定的，不是为某一条 MMA 指令定的，所以加载时一次性重排，换来每一步都省事。Marlin 论文第 3 节描述的正是这种重排：权重交错存放，一次 16 字节的读取正好装下 4 个不同的 16×16 tensor core 块各自的 8 个权重。动画 (a) 画的就是这个内核的骨架，Lab B 用 Metal 写了一个。

Why repack: a dequantize-as-you-read kernel wants each thread to get the 32 weights it is about to use from one 16-byte load, and wants the dequantized FP16 values to land in exactly the register layout the tensor-core instruction expects, with no shuffling between threads. The packing order in a checkpoint is set for the tool that wrote it, not for any particular MMA instruction, so the weights are repacked once at load time to make every step cheap. Section 3 of the Marlin paper describes exactly this repacking: the weights are interleaved so that one 16-byte read holds the 8 weights of each of 4 separate 16×16 tensor-core blocks. Diagram (a) draws the skeleton of such a kernel, and Lab B writes one in Metal.

4. FP8 W8A8 与块缩放 GEMM

4. FP8 W8A8 and the block-scaled GEMM

DeepSeek-V3 技术报告（arXiv:2412.19437，v2）第 3.3.2 节的细粒度量化是今天 FP8 检查点最常见的格式：激活按 1 × 128 的小片缩放（每个 token 每 128 个通道一个缩放因子），权重按 128 × 128 的块缩放，每个小片和每个块的最大绝对值都在线算出，再得出缩放因子。Qwen/Qwen3-8B-FP8 的 config.json 用的就是这个格式：quant_method fp8、fmt e4m3、weight_block_size [128, 128]、activation_scheme dynamic（2026-11-01 读取），文件共 9,436,628,784 字节。

The fine-grained quantization in section 3.3.2 of the DeepSeek-V3 technical report (arXiv:2412.19437, v2) is the most common format of today’s FP8 checkpoints: activations are scaled in 1 × 128 tiles (one scale per token per 128 channels) and weights in 128 × 128 blocks, with the largest absolute value of each tile and block computed online and the scale derived from it. Qwen/Qwen3-8B-FP8’s config.json uses exactly this format: quant_method fp8, fmt e4m3, weight_block_size [128, 128] and activation_scheme dynamic (read 2026-11-01); its files total 9,436,628,784 bytes.

缩放沿 K 每 128 个就变一次，所以不能等整个点积算完再乘：每走一个 K 块，把 tensor core 算出的部分和乘上这一块的两个缩放，再加进 FP32 累加器。SGLang 的 Triton 实现 _w8a8_block_fp8_matmul（python/sglang/kernels/ops/gemm/fp8_kernel.py 第 55 行）里就是一行：accumulator += tl.dot(a, b) * a_s[:, None] * b_s[None, :]（第 126 行），其中 a_s 是这一块里每行一个，b_s 是每 128 列一个。动画 (b) 一步一步画这个循环。

The scales change every 128 elements along K, so they cannot wait until the whole dot product is done: at every K block, the partial sum from the tensor cores is multiplied by that block’s two scales and added into the FP32 accumulator. In SGLang’s Triton implementation, _w8a8_block_fp8_matmul (python/sglang/kernels/ops/gemm/fp8_kernel.py, line 55), this is a single line: accumulator += tl.dot(a, b) * a_s[:, None] * b_s[None, :] (line 126), where a_s holds one value per row of the block and b_s one per 128 columns. 把一个激活值走一遍量化，再看一个 K = 4096 的点积要换几次缩放。激活的数值是示意值，不来自任何模型；448 是 E4M3 的最大值（概念 1），128 是 weight_block_size 和激活小片的宽度。

Walk one activation value through quantization, then count how many times a K = 4096 dot product changes scales. The activation values are illustrative and come from no model; 448 is the largest E4M3 value (concept 1), and 128 is the weight_block_size and the width of an activation tile.

tile max |x| = 3.5 (illustrative)  -> a_s = 3.5 / 448 = 0.0078125
x = 1.2 -> 1.2 / a_s = 153.6 -> nearest E4M3 is 160 (step 16 in [128, 256))
dequantized: 160 x 0.0078125 = 1.25, error 0.05
K = 4096 -> 4096 / 128 = 32 K blocks: 32 new a_s, 32 new b_s, 32 promotions
  1. 在线量化：这个 token 在这 128 个通道上的最大绝对值是 3.5，缩放取 3.5 / 448，让最大的那个数正好映到 E4M3 的 448（图解 (b) 第 1 步）。
  2. 1.2 除以缩放是 153.6。E4M3 只有 3 位尾数，128 到 256 之间每隔 16 一个值，最近的是 160，存进 1 字节。
  3. 用回来时 160 乘缩放得 1.25，误差 0.05，约 4%。E4M3 是浮点格式，只要一个数落在正常范围里，相对误差就由 3 位尾数决定，最多约 6%，和缩放取多大关系不大；按 1 × 128 小片缩放的意义在于，别处的一个离群值不会决定这 128 个数的缩放，它们当中小的数就不会被压到 E4M3 最小的正常值 2-6 以下，在那里精度变差，甚至舍成 0。
  4. K = 4096 的点积分成 32 个 K 块，每块的部分和都要乘上这一块的 a_s 和 b_s 再加进 FP32 累加器（图解 (b) 第 4、5 步）；按 DeepSeek-V3 的做法，每 128 个元素提升一次，正好也是 32 次。
  1. Online quantization: this token’s largest absolute value over these 128 channels is 3.5, so the scale is 3.5 / 448, which maps that largest value exactly onto E4M3’s 448 (step 1 of diagram (b)).
  2. 1.2 divided by the scale is 153.6. E4M3 has only 3 mantissa bits, so between 128 and 256 there is one value every 16; the nearest is 160, stored in one byte.
  3. Used again, 160 times the scale is 1.25, an error of 0.05, about 4%. E4M3 is a floating-point format, so as long as a value lands in its normal range, the relative error is set by the 3 mantissa bits, at most about 6%, whatever the scale; what 1 × 128 tiles buy is that an outlier elsewhere does not set the scale for these 128 values, so their small entries are not pushed below E4M3’s smallest normal value, 2-6, where precision falls away and values can round to 0.
  4. The K = 4096 dot product splits into 32 K blocks, and each block’s partial sum is multiplied by that block’s a_s and b_s before it is added into the FP32 accumulator (steps 4 and 5 of diagram (b)); with DeepSeek-V3’s scheme the sum is promoted every 128 elements, also exactly 32 times.

累加的精度。报告同一节说，他们观察到 H800 上 FP8 GEMM 的累加只保留约 14 位，K = 4096 的两个随机矩阵相乘最大相对误差接近 2%；办法是把部分和每隔 NC = 128 个元素（相当于 4 次 WGMMA）拷到 CUDA core 上的 FP32 寄存器里累加，按 K 分组的缩放也在这时一并乘上；一个 warpgroup 做提升时另一个可以继续做 MMA，两者重叠。块缩放和提升恰好对齐：每 128 个元素既换一次缩放，也提升一次。

Accumulation precision. The same section of the report says they observed that FP8 GEMM accumulation on H800s keeps only about 14 bits, giving a maximum relative error of nearly 2% for two random matrices with K = 4096; the fix is to copy the partial sums every NC = 128 elements (equivalent to 4 WGMMAs) into FP32 registers on the CUDA cores and accumulate there, applying the per-group scales along K at the same time; while one warpgroup promotes, another keeps issuing MMAs, so the two overlap. The block scales and the promotion line up exactly: every 128 elements, the scale changes and the sum is promoted once.

5. NVFP4 and MXFP4 on Blackwell

FP4 的块缩放 MMA 是 Blackwell 才有的指令。PTX ISA（2026-11-01 读取）：tcgen05.mma 的 .kind::mxf4nvf4 和 .kind::mxf4 支持 sm_100a、sm_103a、sm_107a 和 sm_110a（sm_101a 已改名为 sm_110a）；warp 级 mma 的同样两种 kind 支持 sm_120a 和 sm_121a，E2M1 等更窄类型的 mma 要求 sm_120a。缩放不是由软件在累加之后乘上去的，而是作为操作数和元素一起交给指令：.scale_vec::2X 配 UE8M0（MXFP4，每 32 个元素一个），.scale_vec::4X 配 UE4M3（NVFP4，每 16 个元素一个）。这就是概念 4 那一行 Triton 代码在硬件里的样子。

Block-scaled FP4 MMA is an instruction that only Blackwell has. The PTX ISA (read 2026-11-01): .kind::mxf4nvf4 and .kind::mxf4 of tcgen05.mma are supported on sm_100a, sm_103a, sm_107a and sm_110a (sm_101a was renamed sm_110a); the same two kinds of warp-level mma are supported on sm_120a and sm_121a, and mma on E2M1 and the other narrower types requires sm_120a. The scales are not multiplied in by software after accumulation; they are handed to the instruction as operands alongside the elements: .scale_vec::2X with UE8M0 (MXFP4, one per 32 elements) and .scale_vec::4X with UE4M3 (NVFP4, one per 16 elements). 6. CUTLASS、CuTe DSL 与 DeepGEMM

6. CUTLASS, the CuTe DSL and DeepGEMM

上面每种格式都要一个专门的 GEMM：数据类型、缩放的放法、tile 形状、流水线级数和用哪条 MMA 指令都不同，一一手写不现实。CUTLASS 就是用来拼这些 GEMM 的库。它的 README（标签 v4.8.1，2026-10-21 发布，2026-11-01 读取）说，CUTLASS 自 2017 年起提供高性能线性代数的 CUDA C++ 模板抽象，支持 FP64 到 FP16、BF16，8 位浮点（e5m2 与 e4m3），块缩放类型（NVIDIA NVFP4 和 OCP 标准的 MXFP4、MXFP6、MXFP8），4 位和 8 位整数，覆盖 Volta 到 Blackwell；CUTLASS 4 加入了 CUTLASS DSL，第一个是 CuTe DSL，一个与 CuTe C++ 抽象完全一致、暴露 layout、tensor、硬件 atom 和线程与数据层级的 Python 原生底层编程模型。README 说 CuTe DSL 演示的是面向 Ampere、Hopper 和 Blackwell tensor core 的矩阵乘，目前处于公开 beta，预计 2026 年夏末结束 beta。

Every format above needs its own GEMM: the data types, the scale layout, the tile shape, the pipeline depth and the MMA instruction all differ, and writing each by hand is not realistic. CUTLASS is the library for assembling these GEMMs. Its README (the v4.8.1 tag, released 2026-10-21, read 2026-11-01) says CUTLASS has provided CUDA C++ template abstractions for high-performance linear algebra since 2017, supporting FP64 down to FP16 and BF16, 8-bit floating point (e5m2 and e4m3), block-scaled types (NVIDIA NVFP4 and the OCP-standard MXFP4, MXFP6 and MXFP8), and 4- and 8-bit integers, across Volta to Blackwell; CUTLASS 4 adds the CUTLASS DSLs, the first of them the CuTe DSL, a Python-native low-level programming model fully consistent with the CuTe C++ abstractions that exposes layouts, tensors, hardware atoms and the thread and data hierarchy. The README says the CuTe DSL demonstrates matrix multiplies targeting the tensor cores of Ampere, Hopper and Blackwell, and is currently in public beta, expected to leave beta by the end of summer 2026.

CuTe DSL 的快速入门（docs.nvidia.com，页面标注 2026-10-16 更新，2026-11-01 读取）说 4.8 版支持 Linux 和 Windows 上的 Python 3.10 到 3.14t，CUDA 13 用 pip install "nvidia-cutlass-dsl[cu13]" 安装。v4.8.1 标签下的示例里有：Ampere 的 cute/ampere/kernel/dense_gemm/tensorop_gemm.py（文档字符串说支持 fp16、bf16 和 fp8 输入，FP8 用 16 × 8 × 32 的 MMA，默认 tile 128 × 128 × 64），Hopper 的 dense_gemm_fp8_2xacc.py，Blackwell 的 blockscaled_gemm/dense_blockscaled_gemm_persistent.py、blockwise_gemm/blockwise_gemm.py 和 tutorial_gemm/nvfp4_gemm_0.py。Lab A 第 4 步在 L4 上跑第一个。SGLang v0.5.22 依赖 nvidia-cutlass-dsl[cu13]==4.8.0（python/pyproject.toml 第 55 行），比本页引用的文档低一个补丁版本；SGLang 里用 CuTe DSL 写的路径有 --fp8-gemm-backend flashinfer_cutedsl（SM100/SM103 上的 MXFP8）和 --bf16-gemm-backend cutedsl（SM10x），见 exec_.py 第 223–248 行的帮助文字。

DeepGEMM（README，标签 v2.1.1.post3，2026-11-01 读取）走的是另一条路：README 说它借用了 CUTLASS 和 CuTe 的一些概念，但不重度依赖它们的模板或代数，只有少数几个核心内核函数，所以也适合拿来学习 NVIDIA GPU 的内核优化；所有内核在运行时由一个轻量的 JIT 模块编译，安装时不编译。要求 SM90 或 SM100，SM90 要 CUDA 12.3 以上（推荐 12.9 以上），SM100 要 12.9 以上，以及 CUTLASS 4.0 以上。接口按 D = C + A @ B 命名，NT 布局；左操作数的缩放因子要 TMA 对齐并转置，SM90 要 FP32 缩放，SM100 要把 4 个 UE8M0 打包进一个 torch.int。README 说输入的转置和 FP8 转换要由使用者自己做或融进前面的内核，库只专注于 GEMM 本身。分组 GEMM 的 contiguous 和 masked 两种布局见 Phase 4。README 报告在 H800 上最高 1550 TFLOPS，这是作者的数字，本页没有复现。

DeepGEMM (README at the v2.1.1.post3 tag, read 2026-11-01) takes a different road: the README says it leverages some concepts from CUTLASS and CuTe but avoids heavy reliance on their templates or algebras, with only a limited number of core kernel functions, which also makes it a good resource for learning NVIDIA GPU kernel optimization; all kernels are compiled at run time by a lightweight JIT module, with nothing compiled at install time. The README says transposing inputs and casting to FP8 are left to the user, to be done or fused into earlier kernels, and that the library focuses on the GEMM itself. The contiguous and masked layouts of its grouped GEMMs are covered in Phase 4. The README reports up to 1550 TFLOPS on an H800; that is the authors’ figure, not reproduced here.

  • Every quantization scheme is a low-precision number times a scale, and they differ in how many numbers one scale covers: per tensor, per channel, per token, per group of 128 or per 128 × 128 block.
  • Decode GEMVs are bandwidth-bound, so quantization saves time only when the kernel actually reads fewer bytes; expanding the weights back to FP16 in memory first costs 4.5 bytes per weight and is slower than FP16.
  • Fused int4 reads 67 bytes per 128 weights against 256 for FP16, about 3.8 times fewer, and Lab B measured 3.84 times as fast; Marlin needs sm_80 or later in SGLang and repacks the weights at load time.
  • Block-scaled FP8 changes scales every 128 elements along K, so every K block’s partial sum is multiplied by both scales before it enters the FP32 accumulator; FP8 mma needs sm_89 or later.
  • The native MMAs for NVFP4 (one E4M3 scale per 16) and MXFP4 (one UE8M0 scale per 32) exist only on Blackwell, and an NVFP4 checkpoint loaded on an L4 or an H100 runs through Marlin W4A16.

常见误解

Common misconceptions

  • 误解：检查点小了 4 倍，解码就快 4 倍。→ 实际：只有配套的内核边读边解时才快；SGLang 强制 --quantization awq 时走先解后算，Lab B 同样的做法只有 FP16 的 0.44 倍。
  • 误解：FP8 检查点在任何 GPU 上都用 FP8 tensor core。→ 实际：FP8 的 mma 要 sm_89；A100 这类 sm_80 的卡上 SGLang 把它改走 Marlin 的 FP8 仅权重内核，只省带宽。
  • 误解：缩放可以等整个点积算完再乘一次。→ 实际：块缩放沿 K 每 128 个就变，必须在每个 K 块内乘上；只有每张量或每通道的缩放才能放到最后。
  • Myth: a checkpoint 4 times smaller decodes 4 times faster. → Fact: only with a matching kernel that dequantizes as it reads; a forced --quantization awq in SGLang takes the dequantize-first path, and the same approach in Lab B ran at only 0.44 times FP16.
  • Myth: an FP8 checkpoint uses FP8 tensor cores on any GPU. → Fact: FP8 mma needs sm_89; on sm_80 cards such as the A100, SGLang moves it onto Marlin’s FP8 weight-only kernel, which saves only bandwidth.
  • Myth: the scales can be applied once after the whole dot product. → Fact: block scales change every 128 elements along K and must be applied inside each K block; only per-tensor or per-channel scales can wait until the end.

kernel void gemv_f16(device const half* W [[buffer(0)]], device const half* x [[buffer(1)]],
                     device float* y [[buffer(2)]], constant uint& K [[buffer(3)]],
                     uint tg [[threadgroup_position_in_grid]], uint sg [[simdgroup_index_in_threadgroup]],
                     uint lane [[thread_index_in_simdgroup]]) {
    uint row = tg * 8 + sg;
    device const half4* w = (device const half4*)(W + ulong(row) * K);
    device const half4* xv = (device const half4*)x;
    float acc = 0.0f;
    for (uint i = lane * 2; i < K / 4; i += 64) {
        acc += dot(float4(w[i]), float4(xv[i])) + dot(float4(w[i + 1]), float4(xv[i + 1]));
    }
    acc = simd_sum(acc);
    if (lane == 0) y[row] = acc;
}

// y = sum over groups of s_g * sum(q * x): the scale is applied once per 16 weights.

                    device const half* S [[buffer(4)]],
                    uint tg [[threadgroup_position_in_grid]], uint sg [[simdgroup_index_in_threadgroup]],

    device const char4* w = (device const char4*)(W + ulong(row) * K);

            default:   // unfused: dequantize to FP16 in memory, then the FP16 GEMV reads it back
                enc.setComputePipelineState(pDq); enc.setBuffer(c4[c], offset: 0, index: 0); enc.setBuffer(s4, offset: 0, index: 4)
                enc.setBuffer(z4, offset: 0, index: 5); enc.setBuffer(scratch, offset: 0, index: 6)
                enc.dispatchThreads(MTLSize(width: nk / 8, height: 1, depth: 1), threadsPerThreadgroup: MTLSize(width: 256, height: 1, depth: 1))
                enc.setComputePipelineState(pF16); enc.setBuffer(scratch, offset: 0, index: 0); enc.setBuffer(yU, offset: 0, index: 2)
            }
            enc.setBuffer(xB, offset: 0, index: 1); enc.setBytes(&kk, length: 4, index: 3)
            enc.dispatchThreadgroups(MTLSize(width: N / 8, height: 1, depth: 1), threadsPerThreadgroup: MTLSize(width: 256, height: 1, depth: 1))
        }
        enc.endEncoding(); cb.commit(); cb.waitUntilCompleted()
        return (cb.gpuEndTime - cb.gpuStartTime) * 1e6 / Double(C)      // microseconds per GEMV
    }
    var us = [Double](repeating: 0, count: 4)
    for f in 0..<4 { _ = run(f); us[f] = median((0..<21).map { _ in run(f) }) }

    // checks on 64 sampled rows: each GPU result against a Double reference of the same format
    let gF = yF.contents().bindMemory(to: Float.self, capacity: N)
    let g8 = y8.contents().bindMemory(to: Float.self, capacity: N)
    let g4 = y4.contents().bindMemory(to: Float.self, capacity: N)
    let gU = yU.contents().bindMemory(to: Float.self, capacity: N)
    var errs = [Double](repeating: 0, count: 4), refMax = 0.0
    for r in stride(from: 0, to: N, by: N / 64) {
        var rf = 0.0, r8 = 0.0, r4 = 0.0
        for k in 0..<K {
            let i = r * K + k, g = i / G, xv = Double(px[k])
            rf += Double(pF[i]) * xv
            r8 += Double(p8[i]) * Double(ps8[g]) * xv
            let q = Double((p4[i / 8] >> (4 * UInt32(i % 8))) & 0xF)
            r4 += (q - Double(pz4[g])) * Double(ps4[g]) * xv
        }
        refMax = max(refMax, abs(rf))
        errs[0] = max(errs[0], abs(Double(gF[r]) - rf)); errs[1] = max(errs[1], abs(Double(g8[r]) - r8)); errs[2] = max(errs[2], abs(Double(g4[r]) - r4))
        errs[3] = max(errs[3], abs(Double(gU[r]) - r4))
    }
    // quantization error: int8 / int4 GPU outputs against the FP16 GPU output, all N rows (relative RMS)
    var d8 = 0.0, d4 = 0.0, nf = 0.0
    for r in 0..<N { let f = Double(gF[r]); nf += f * f; d8 += pow(Double(g8[r]) - f, 2); d4 += pow(Double(g4[r]) - f, 2) }
    let names = ["fp16", "int8", "int4", "int4_unfused"]
    let bytes = [f16Bytes, i8Bytes, i4Bytes, i4Bytes + 2 * f16Bytes]   // unfused: read int4, write FP16, read FP16
    print(String(format: "N=%d K=%d  copies=%d  fp16 weights %.1f MB", N, K, C, Double(f16Bytes) / 1e6))
    for f in 0..<4 {
        let gbs = Double(bytes[f]) / (us[f] * 1e-6) / 1e9
        let q = f == 0 || f == 3 ? "" : String(format: "  quant_err_vs_fp16=%.2e", sqrt((f == 1 ? d8 : d4) / nf))
        print(String(format: "  %@  bytes=%7.1f MB  median_us=%8.1f  GB/s=%6.1f  speedup_vs_fp16=%.2fx  max_abs_err_vs_ref=%.1e (ref max %.1f)%@",
                     names[f].padding(toLength: 12, withPad: " ", startingAt: 0), Double(bytes[f]) / 1e6, us[f], gbs, us[0] / us[f], errs[f], refMax, q))
    }
}

1. 在 L4 上用 --quantization awq 强制加载 Qwen/Qwen3-8B-AWQ，权重只有 BF16 原模型的约 37%，解码却比 BF16 还慢。主要原因是什么？

1. On an L4, Qwen/Qwen3-8B-AWQ is loaded with --quantization awq forced. Its weights are about 37% of the BF16 model’s, yet it decodes more slowly than BF16. What is the main reason?

AWQLinearKernel.apply 是 awq_dequantize 加 torch.matmul（awq_kernels.py 第 97–98 行）：读 0.5 字节、写 2 字节、再读 2 字节，Lab B 量到这种做法只有 FP16 的 0.44 倍，所以 c 正确。a 不对：W4A16 的内核本来就不用 int4 tensor core，Marlin 在同一张卡上把 int4 解成 FP16 喂给 FP16 tensor core，照样快。b 不对：AWQ 是仅权重量化，激活保持 FP16，AWQConfig 支持的激活类型只有 torch.float16。AWQLinearKernel.apply is awq_dequantize plus torch.matmul (awq_kernels.py, lines 97–98): 0.5 bytes read, 2 written and 2 read again, and Lab B measured this approach at only 0.44 times FP16, so c is right. a is wrong: W4A16 kernels never use int4 tensor cores; on the same card Marlin dequantizes int4 to FP16 for the FP16 tensor cores and is fast. b is wrong: AWQ is weight-only, the activations stay FP16, and the only activation type AWQConfig supports is torch.float16.

2. 在 FP8 块缩放 GEMM 里，为什么 a_s 和 b_s 要在 K 循环的每一步里乘上，而不是等点积全部算完再乘一次？

2. In an FP8 block-scaled GEMM, why are a_s and b_s applied at every step of the K loop rather than once after the whole dot product?

激活是每 token 每 128 通道一个缩放，权重是每 128 × 128 一个，所以沿 K 的每个 128 宽的块都有不同的 a_s 和 b_s，Σk sk · pk 不能写成 s · Σk pk，a 正确。b 不对：E4M3 的最大值只有 448，128 个乘积之和远在 FP32 的范围之内，问题是缩放不同，不是溢出。c 不对：sm_89 和 Hopper 的 FP8 mma 不带缩放操作数，所以 Triton 才要在软件里乘；把缩放作为操作数交给指令的是 Blackwell 的块缩放 MMA。Activations have one scale per token per 128 channels and weights one per 128 × 128, so every 128-wide block along K has different a_s and b_s, and Σk sk · pk cannot be written as s · Σk pk; a is right. b is wrong: the largest E4M3 value is only 448, and a sum of 128 products is well within FP32’s range; the issue is that the scales differ, not overflow. c is wrong: the FP8 mma of sm_89 and Hopper takes no scale operands, which is exactly why Triton applies them in software; handing scales to the instruction as operands is what Blackwell’s block-scaled MMA does.

3. 在 A100（sm_80）上用 SGLang v0.5.22 加载 Qwen/Qwen3-8B-FP8，线性层实际怎么算？

3. On an A100 (sm_80), SGLang v0.5.22 loads Qwen/Qwen3-8B-FP8. How are its linear layers actually computed?

Fp8LinearMethod 在 can_auto_enable_marlin_fp8() 为真时打开 Marlin（fp8.py 第 506–512 行），而这个判断就是 80 <= sm < 89（fp8_utils.py 第 2540–2546 行），所以 b 正确。a 不对：DeepGEMM 只在 sm_90 以上启用，而且 PTX ISA 说 FP8 的 mma 要 sm_89，A100 根本没有 FP8 tensor core。c 不对：Fp8Config 的最低计算能力是 80（fp8.py 第 323–333 行），最低 89 的是 w8a8_fp8。Fp8LinearMethod turns on Marlin when can_auto_enable_marlin_fp8() is true (fp8.py, lines 506–512), and that test is exactly 80 <= sm < 89 (fp8_utils.py, lines 2540–2546), so b is right. a is wrong: DeepGEMM is enabled only from sm_90, and the PTX ISA says FP8 mma needs sm_89, so an A100 has no FP8 tensor cores at all. c is wrong: Fp8Config has a minimum compute capability of 80 (fp8.py, lines 323–333); the method with a minimum of 89 is w8a8_fp8.

4. Lab B 里 int4 GEMV 比 FP16 快约 3.82–3.84 倍，而不是 4 倍。最主要的原因是什么？

4. In Lab B the int4 GEMV is about 3.82–3.84 times as fast as FP16, not 4 times. What is the main reason?

四个内核的有效带宽都在约 127–128 GB/s，int4 与 FP16 几乎相同，说明 int4 仍然受带宽限制，解包的指令藏在访存后面，所以 a 不对。b 不对：程序复制了 C 份权重，使 FP16 合计至少 512 MB，而且如果有缓存效应，int4 的带宽应当明显高于 FP16，测量里没有。c 正确：按字节算 2 / (0.5 + 3/128) ≈ 3.82，与实测吻合。All four kernels run at an effective 127–128 GB/s, int4 almost exactly like FP16, which shows that int4 is still bandwidth-bound and the unpacking instructions hide behind the memory traffic, so a is wrong. b is wrong: the program makes C copies of the weights so that the FP16 copies total at least 512 MB, and a cache effect would show int4 at a clearly higher bandwidth than FP16, which the measurement does not. c is right: by bytes, 2 / (0.5 + 3/128) ≈ 3.82, matching the measurement.
