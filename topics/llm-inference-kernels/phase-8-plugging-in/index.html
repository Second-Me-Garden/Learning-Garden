核心概念

Core concepts

前七个阶段的实验里写过的内核，不进引擎就只是一个独立的基准。进引擎有两道坎。第一道是从哪个口子进、守什么契约：口子选错，SGLang 根本调不到你的算子；契约没守住，它在解码图捕获时就出错。第二道是进去之后引擎到底快了多少：Lab B 里单独快 1.87 倍的融合算子，放进一个 17.3 ms 的解码步，只省下 0.2% 左右。所以问题是：一个写好的内核怎样被 SGLang 调到，又怎样证明它让端到端变快？

The kernels written in the labs of the first seven phases are only standalone benchmarks until they enter the engine. Getting in has two hurdles. The first is which opening to use and which contract to keep: pick the wrong opening and SGLang never calls your op; break the contract and it fails when the decode graph is captured. The second is how much faster the engine actually gets: in Lab B a fused op that is 1.87 times as fast on its own saves only about 0.2% of a 17.3 ms decode step. So the question is: how does a finished kernel get called by SGLang, and how do you prove it makes the engine faster end to end?

Concepts 2 and 3 answer the first half (the two openings, a torch op in the kernel library and an AttentionBackend, with their contracts), and concept 5 answers the second half with four tools and Amdahl’s law.

1. 四个接入点在栈上的位置

1. Where the four plug-in points sit on the stack

简报的四层栈是前端、运行时（SRT）、模型执行器和内核层。内核工程师几乎不碰前两层，接入点都在后两层和它们下面：

The brief’s stack has four layers: the frontend, the runtime (SRT), the model executor and the kernel layer. A kernel engineer almost never touches the first two; the plug-in points are all in the last two and below them:

  • 内核库里的一个算子：放在 kernels/ 下。提前编译的 CUDA/C++ 在 kernels/aot/（PyPI 包 sglang-kernel，Python 模块名仍是 sgl_kernel，kernels/aot/README.md 第 12 行），注册成 torch.ops.sgl_kernel.*；即时编译的在 kernels/jit/；对 SGLang 其他代码公开的调用入口一律在 kernels/ops/<组>/。模型执行器里的层（例如 srt/layers/layernorm.py）调用它。概念 2。
  • An op in the kernel library: under kernels/. Ahead-of-time CUDA/C++ lives in kernels/aot/ (PyPI package sglang-kernel, Python module still sgl_kernel, line 12 of kernels/aot/README.md) and registers as torch.ops.sgl_kernel.*; just-in-time builds live in kernels/jit/; the public call surface for the rest of SGLang is always kernels/ops/<group>/. Layers in the model executor (for example srt/layers/layernorm.py) call it. Concept 2.
  • 一个 AttentionBackend：在 srt/layers/attention/，夹在模型执行器和注意力内核之间。它把运行时的批（哪些请求、各自的 KV 在哪些槽位）翻译成内核要的索引数组，再调内核。概念 3。
  • An AttentionBackend: in srt/layers/attention/, between the model executor and the attention kernel. It translates the runtime’s batch (which requests, where each one’s KV sits) into the index arrays the kernel wants, then calls the kernel. Concept 3.
  • 上游库：FlashInfer、DeepGEMM、FlashMLA、CUTLASS 和 CuTe DSL 在 SGLang 仓库之外，以钉住版本的依赖进入（python/pyproject.toml）。概念 4。
  • The upstream libraries: FlashInfer, DeepGEMM, FlashMLA, CUTLASS and the CuTe DSL live outside the SGLang repository and come in as pinned dependencies (python/pyproject.toml). Concept 4.
  • 工具：不属于任何一层，但每一层的改动都要靠它们证明。概念 5。写内核用什么语言是概念 6。
  • The tools: they belong to no layer, but every change on any layer is proved with them. Concept 5. The languages kernels are written in are concept 6.

kernels/README.md 定下了一条评审规则（第 164–172 行）：SGLang 的运行时代码和测试应当从 sglang.kernels.ops.* 导入可调用的内核；实现可以继续写在 sglang.kernels.jit 或 sgl_kernel 里，但新加一个可调用内核的 PR 要同时给它加一个 sglang.kernels.ops.* 入口。分组按「公开的计算」分，不按最先用它的模型分（第 54–58 行）。

Groups follow the public computation, not the model that first used it (lines 54–58).

3. 围绕一个内核实现 AttentionBackend

3. An AttentionBackend around a kernel

srt/layers/attention/base_attn_backend.py 第 36 行的 AttentionBackend 是抽象基类。它的文档字符串（第 39–56 行）把「准备前向数据」写成三个方法的契约：

AttentionBackend at line 36 of srt/layers/attention/base_attn_backend.py is an abstract base class. Its docstring (lines 39–56) states the contract for preparing forward data as three methods:

  • init_forward_metadata(fb)（第 88 行）：急切执行时的入口，默认先调 _out_graph 再调 _in_graph。
  • init_forward_metadata(fb) (line 88): the eager entry point; by default it calls _out_graph and then _in_graph.

把内核时间换算成端到端，用的是阿姆达尔定律：一个占整步比例 f 的部分快了 s 倍，整步只快 1 / ((1 − f) + f / s) 倍。32 的数法：OLMoE 16 层，每层 2 处归一化，第 0 层的输入归一化没有残差、不算，最后一个归一化带残差、算上，合计 32 处残差 + RMSNorm；Lab B 的替身每层直接跑 2 处，总数相同。替身里，这 32 处按批大小 1 的单独测量合计约 60–120 µs，占 17.3 ms 一步的约 0.4–0.7%（推断，按 Lab B 第一部分的中位数相乘）；就算它们快到零，整步也只快约 0.7%。所以作品项目要求报告先给出这个预测，再给出测量值。

Converting kernel time into end-to-end time is Amdahl’s law: if a part that takes a fraction f of the step becomes s times faster, the step becomes only 1 / ((1 − f) + f / s) times faster. The 32 is counted this way: OLMoE has 16 layers with 2 norms each; layer 0’s input norm has no residual and is left out, and the final norm has one and is counted, giving 32 residual + RMSNorm sites; Lab B’s stand-in simply runs 2 per layer, the same total. In the stand-in, those 32 sites, measured alone at batch size 1, add up to about 60–120 µs, about 0.4–0.7% of a 17.3 ms step (inference, multiplying the medians of Lab B’s first part); even if they took no time at all, the step would be at most about 0.7% faster. That is why the capstone asks the report to give this prediction first and the measurement second.

32 sites   = 32 x 3.84 us                      = 122.9 us

  2. 代进阿姆达尔定律：整步快 1.0033 倍，也就是省 57.3 µs，正是程序打印的 predicted from Part 1: 32 sites x 1.79 us = 57.3 us。
  3. 再看上限：就算融合版快到不花时间，整步也最多少 122.9 µs，即 0.71%，因为其余 99.29% 的时间不归归一化管，几乎全花在读权重上。

  2. Put them into Amdahl’s law: the step becomes 1.0033 times as fast, a saving of 57.3 µs, exactly the predicted from Part 1: 32 sites x 1.79 us = 57.3 us the program prints.

常见误解

Common misconceptions

  • 误解：单独快 1.87 倍的融合算子，放进引擎也会让解码明显变快。→ 实际：Lab B 的替身整步只快 0.14–0.24%，因为这一步几乎全在读 2.15 GB 权重，32 处归一化本来只占约 0.4–0.7%。
  • 误解：Nsight Systems 的 trace 里找不到你的内核，说明它没换进去。→ 实际：--cuda-graph-trace 默认是 graph，整张图只记一项；加 --cuda-graph-trace=node 或关掉解码图才看得到单个内核。
  • 误解：在上游 FlashInfer 合进去的改动，下次装 SGLang 就有了。→ 实际：v0.5.22 钉的是 flashinfer_python[cu13]==0.7.1，要等 SGLang 把钉住的版本挪过去才生效。
  • Myth: a fused op that is 1.87 times as fast on its own will make decode noticeably faster inside the engine. → Fact: Lab B’s stand-in step is only 0.14–0.24% faster, because the step goes almost entirely on reading 2.15 GB of weights and the 32 norm sites took only about 0.4–0.7% of it.
  • Myth: if your kernel is missing from the Nsight Systems trace, it was not swapped in. → Fact: --cuda-graph-trace defaults to graph, which records a whole graph as one item; add --cuda-graph-trace=node or turn the decode graph off to see individual kernels.
  • Myth: a change merged into upstream FlashInfer is there the next time you install SGLang. → Fact: v0.5.22 pins flashinfer_python[cu13]==0.7.1, so the change takes effect only after SGLang moves the pin.

#include <ATen/cuda/CUDAContext.h>
#include <c10/cuda/CUDAGuard.h>
#include <cuda_bf16.h>
#include <torch/all.h>
#include <torch/library.h>

constexpr int kThreads = 256, kMaxPerThread = 16;   // hidden size up to 256 x 16 = 4096

// One block per token row. Each thread keeps its part of the sum in registers between the two passes.
// shortcut: scalar BF16 loads; the capstone asks you to vectorize them to 16 bytes and measure again.
__global__ void fused_add_rmsnorm_kernel(__nv_bfloat16* __restrict__ x, __nv_bfloat16* __restrict__ r,
                                         const __nv_bfloat16* __restrict__ w, int h, float eps) {
  __shared__ float part[kThreads / 32];
  const int tid = threadIdx.x;
  __nv_bfloat16* xr = x + static_cast<int64_t>(blockIdx.x) * h;
  __nv_bfloat16* rr = r + static_cast<int64_t>(blockIdx.x) * h;
  float v[kMaxPerThread];
  float ss = 0.f;
#pragma unroll
  for (int k = 0; k < kMaxPerThread; ++k) {
    const int j = tid + k * kThreads;
    if (j < h) {
      const __nv_bfloat16 s = __float2bfloat16(__bfloat162float(xr[j]) + __bfloat162float(rr[j]));
      rr[j] = s;                                      // the new residual: written once, never read back
      v[k] = __bfloat162float(s);
      ss += v[k] * v[k];
    }
  }
  for (int o = 16; o > 0; o >>= 1) ss += __shfl_xor_sync(0xffffffffu, ss, o);   // warp sum
  if (tid % 32 == 0) part[tid / 32] = ss;
  __syncthreads();
  float total = 0.f;
#pragma unroll
  for (int i = 0; i < kThreads / 32; ++i) total += part[i];                     // block sum
  const float inv = rsqrtf(total / h + eps);
#pragma unroll
  for (int k = 0; k < kMaxPerThread; ++k) {
    const int j = tid + k * kThreads;
    if (j < h) xr[j] = __float2bfloat16(v[k] * inv * __bfloat162float(w[j]));
  }
}

void fused_add_rmsnorm(at::Tensor& input, at::Tensor& residual, at::Tensor& weight, double eps) {
  TORCH_CHECK(input.is_cuda() && input.dim() == 2 && input.is_contiguous(), "input: contiguous 2-D CUDA tensor");
  TORCH_CHECK(input.scalar_type() == at::kBFloat16, "input: BF16 only");
  TORCH_CHECK(residual.sizes() == input.sizes() && residual.scalar_type() == input.scalar_type()
              && residual.is_contiguous(), "residual: same shape and dtype as input, contiguous");
  TORCH_CHECK(weight.dim() == 1 && weight.size(0) == input.size(1) && weight.scalar_type() == input.scalar_type(),
              "weight: [hidden_size], same dtype as input");
  TORCH_CHECK(input.size(1) <= kThreads * kMaxPerThread, "hidden size up to 4096");
  if (input.size(0) == 0) return;
  const at::cuda::OptionalCUDAGuard guard(device_of(input));
  const cudaStream_t stream = at::cuda::getCurrentCUDAStream();     // the stream SGLang captures into its decode graph
  fused_add_rmsnorm_kernel<<<input.size(0), kThreads, 0, stream>>>(
      reinterpret_cast<__nv_bfloat16*>(input.data_ptr()), reinterpret_cast<__nv_bfloat16*>(residual.data_ptr()),
      reinterpret_cast<const __nv_bfloat16*>(weight.data_ptr()), static_cast<int>(input.size(1)), static_cast<float>(eps));
  C10_CUDA_KERNEL_LAUNCH_CHECK();
}

        ts.append(a.elapsed_time(b) * 1000 / reps)
    ts = sorted(ts[3:])

constant uint TG = 256;                      // threads per row

inline float tg_sum(float v, threadgroup float* part, uint tid) {

}

                device half4* r_out [[buffer(2)]], uint i [[thread_position_in_grid]]) {
    r_out[i] = half4(float4(x[i]) + float4(r[i]));
}

kernel void rmsnorm(device const half4* r_out [[buffer(0)]], device const half4* w [[buffer(1)]],

2. 你的新注意力后端在 init_forward_metadata_in_graph 里写了 n = int(seq_lens.max().item())，再按 n 分配一个缓冲区。按基类的契约，该怎么改？

2. Your new attention backend’s init_forward_metadata_in_graph does n = int(seq_lens.max().item()) and then allocates a buffer of size n. By the base class’s contract, how should it change?

第 124–126 行的 lint 契约禁止 _in_graph 调 .item() 和动态形状的 torch.empty()：捕获时录下的那个值在重放时不会更新，下一批更长就会越界。主机操作和动态形状属于 _out_graph，图要用的缓冲区在 init_cuda_graph_state 里按最大批预先分配。forward_decode 同样在解码图里被捕获，挪过去问题照旧。The lint contract at lines 124–126 forbids .item() and dynamic-shape torch.empty() in _in_graph: the value recorded at capture is not updated at replay, so a longer batch later overruns it. 3. Lab B 里，融合的残差 + RMSNorm 在批大小 1 时单独量快 1.40–1.88 倍，放进解码步替身后整步只快 0.14–0.24%。最好的解释是什么？

3. In Lab B, the fused residual + RMSNorm measured alone at batch size 1 is 1.40–1.88 times as fast, yet inside the decode-step stand-in the whole step is only 0.14–0.24% faster. What is the best explanation?

The driver does not merge the two kernels: the saving is not zero, and both dispatches are in the command buffer. Timing resolution is not the issue either: Part 1 uses the same timing to measure single kernels of about 1.4 µs.

4. 你用 nsys profile 录 one_batch 的解码，CUDA 图开着，结果时间线上只看到整张图的执行，看不到你的内核。原因和办法是什么？

4. You record one_batch’s decode with nsys profile, CUDA graphs on, and the timeline shows only whole-graph executions, not your kernel. What is the cause and the fix?

Nsight Systems 用户指南写明默认的 graph 粒度把整张图记成一项、不记节点，node 才记节点并且可能有明显开销。只看到整张图并不说明你的算子没换进去，要用节点粒度或关图后的 trace 才能判断。Nsight Systems 本来就记 CUDA 内核；Nsight Compute 是一次深入一个内核，不是用来列出一步里所有内核的。The Nsight Systems User Guide says the default graph granularity records a whole graph as one item without its nodes, and only node records the nodes, possibly at a significant overhead. Seeing only whole graphs does not show that your op is missing; judge that from a node-granularity trace or one with the graph off. Nsight Systems does record CUDA kernels; Nsight Compute goes deep into one kernel at a time and is not the tool for listing every kernel of a step.

验收标准

Acceptance criteria

  1. 每个 token 数下，V2 和 V4 对 V0 的最大绝对误差不超过 V3 对 V0 误差的两倍；报告里有这个检查的输出。
  2. At every token count, the maximum absolute error of V2 and V4 against V0 is at most twice that of V3 against V0; the report contains the output of this check.
  3. V2 和 V4 通过 torch.library.opcheck，能被捕获进 CUDA 图（Lab A 的 graph_us），并且在 SGLang 默认开着解码图时，节点粒度的 Nsight Systems trace 里每个解码步出现的次数等于你在模型代码里数出的带残差的归一化处数。
  4. V2 and V4 pass torch.library.opcheck, can be captured into a CUDA graph (Lab A’s graph_us), and, with SGLang’s decode graph on as by default, appear in a node-granularity Nsight Systems trace as many times per decode step as the norm sites with a residual that you count in the model code.
  5. 换进 V2 或 V4 之后，one_batch --correctness-test 打印的 logits 和默认设置一致到 BF16 舍入的量级（这个选项会打印 prefill 和 decode 的 logits，见 one_batch.py 第 19–30 行的示例输出）。
  6. With V2 or V4 swapped in, the logits printed by one_batch --correctness-test agree with the default setting to the level of BF16 rounding (the option prints prefill and decode logits; see the sample output at lines 19–30 of one_batch.py).
  7. 每个时间都是至少 3 次预热之后至少 20 次的中位数，带最小值和最大值；GPU 上同时有别的程序的运行要弃用并在报告里说明，像 Lab B 那样。
  8. Every time is the median of at least 20 runs after at least 3 warm-ups, with its minimum and maximum; runs during which something else used the GPU are discarded and reported as such, as in Lab B.
  9. V4 在 4096 个 token 时比 V2 快，或者报告用 ncu 的 DRAM 字节说明为什么没有更快。
  10. At 4096 tokens V4 is faster than V2, or the report uses ncu’s DRAM bytes to explain why it is not.
  11. 端到端的测量落在你事先写下的预测范围里，或者报告用第 2 部分的剖析解释偏差。本页只在 M5 的替身上验证过这一条（测到 24.8–40.8 µs，预测 17.6–57.8 µs）。
  12. The end-to-end measurement falls inside the prediction you wrote down beforehand, or the report explains the deviation with part 2’s profile. This page has verified that only on the M5 stand-in (24.8–40.8 µs measured against 17.6–57.8 µs predicted).
  13. 一个脚本从源码构建所有版本、跑完全部测量、打印报告里的数字；另一个人在同型号的 GPU 和同一个镜像标签上运行，中位数落在你报告的范围附近。
  14. One script builds every version from source, runs every measurement and prints the numbers in the report; someone else running it on the same GPU model and the same image tag gets medians near the ranges you reported.
