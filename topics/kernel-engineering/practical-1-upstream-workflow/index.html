Upstream Workflow & First Patch 上游工作流和第一个补丁：checkpatch、get_maintainer、git send-email、评审与 v2checkpatch, get_maintainer, git send-email, review and a v2

前面九个阶段你一直在读别人的内核代码。这一阶段你要往里面放进一行自己的：找到一处真实的小缺陷，把它做成一个随时可以发给内核邮件列表的补丁，再走完评审和第二版。改动可以只有一行，但每一步都要按真实的流程来做。不把补丁发出去，这一阶段也算完成；发到公开列表由你自己决定，发了就多一条招聘方可以直接查到的公开记录。

For nine phases you have been reading other people’s kernel code. In this stage you put one line of your own into it: find a small real defect, turn it into a patch that is ready for a kernel mailing list, and take it through review and a second version. The change may be a single line, but every step follows the real process. The stage is complete without sending the patch anywhere; posting it to a public list is your decision, and doing so adds a public record that an employer can look up.

任务

The brief

把一个真实的小改动做成一个可以发给内核邮件列表的补丁，并让它经过一轮评审。具体要做到：Turn one small real change into a patch that is ready for a kernel mailing list, and take it through one round of review. In detail:
  • 配好一个能发纯文本补丁的环境：git send-email，以及你的真实姓名和邮箱。
  • Set up an environment that can send plain-text patches: git send-email, with your real name and e-mail address.
  • 在当前的开发树上找到一处真实的小缺陷：文档错误、kernel-doc 警告、或者 drivers/staging 里的清理。
  • Find a small real defect in the current development tree: a documentation error, a kernel-doc warning, or a cleanup in drivers/staging.
  • 写补丁，编译它，用 checkpatch.pl 检查，用 get_maintainer.pl 找到收件人，然后先发给自己检查一遍。
  • Write the patch, build it, check it with checkpatch.pl, find the recipients with get_maintainer.pl, and send it to yourself as a check.
  • 选一条路线收尾。本地路线：隔一天评审自己的补丁，把发现的问题改成第二版。公开路线：真的发到列表，逐段回复评审，对方要求修改时发出第二版。
  • Finish by one of two routes. The local route: a day later, review your own patch and turn what you find into a second version. The public route: really send it to the list, reply to the review inline, and post a second version when one is asked for.
两条路线都算完成这一阶段。公开路线会把你的真实姓名和邮箱永久留在公开的存档里，所以走不走由你本人决定，发信和回信也只能由你本人来做，页面上标着「由学习者完成」。这一页和任何辅助工具都不会替任何人发信。
Either route completes the stage. The public route leaves your real name and e-mail address in a public archive for good, so taking it is your own decision, and the sending and replying are your own acts, marked “done by the learner”. Neither this page nor any assistant tool sends mail for anyone.

交付物与验收标准

Deliverables and acceptance

交付物：补丁文件和半页说明。补丁文件是 git format-patch 的输出（有第二版时两版都交）；说明按本页「怎么写」一节的模板写。走公开路线时再加一个链接，指向 lore.kernel.org 上你的补丁所在的讨论串。

Deliverables: the patch file and half a page of notes. The patch file is the output of git format-patch (both versions when there is a second one); the notes follow the template in “Writing it up” below. On the public route, add one link: the thread on lore.kernel.org that holds your patch.

验收标准：下面六条都成立，这一阶段就算完成。每一条都能由别人对着你交的东西检查。

Acceptance criteria: the stage is done when all six hold. Each can be checked by someone else from what you hand in.

  1. 补丁基于该子系统在 MAINTAINERS 里列出的开发树（T: 行），或者 linux-next；不是基于某个发行版内核或很旧的 tag。
  2. The patch is based on the development tree that MAINTAINERS lists for the subsystem (the T: line), or on linux-next; not on a distribution kernel or an old tag.
  3. scripts/checkpatch.pl 对补丁文件报告 0 errors、0 warnings；如果留下了某条警告，说明里写了理由。
  4. scripts/checkpatch.pl reports 0 errors and 0 warnings on the patch file; if a warning remains, your notes say why.
  5. 你编译过改动的文件，并且在说明里写了用的命令。
  6. You built the files you changed, and your notes give the command.
  7. 收件人与 scripts/get_maintainer.pl 的输出一致：维护者在 To，邮件列表在 Cc。走本地路线时，用 git send-email --dry-run 的输出来证明，这条命令不发出任何邮件。
  8. The recipients match the output of scripts/get_maintainer.pl: maintainers in To, lists in Cc. On the local route this is shown by the output of git send-email --dry-run, a command that sends nothing.
  9. 主题行带有该目录 git log 里通用的前缀，正文用祈使语气写清改了什么、为什么改，末尾有你真实姓名的 Signed-off-by:。
  10. The subject carries the prefix that git log shows for that directory, the body says what changes and why in the imperative mood, and it ends with a Signed-off-by: in your real name.
  11. 补丁经过了一轮评审。本地路线：至少隔一天，你对照前五条重读了自己的补丁，把发现写进了说明；有需要改的地方时，你做了 [PATCH v2]，并在 --- 下面写了与上一版的差别。公开路线：你逐段回复了至少一条评审意见；对方要求修改时，你发了同样格式的 [PATCH v2]。
  12. The patch went through one round of review. Local route: at least a day later you reread your own patch against the first five criteria and wrote what you found into the notes; where something had to change, you made a [PATCH v2] with the differences from the previous version below the ---. Public route: you answered at least one review comment inline, and where a change was asked for you posted a [PATCH v2] in the same form.
走公开路线时，补丁被合并当然更好，但不是验收条件：什么时候合并由维护者决定，不由你控制。发出去之后一个星期没有任何回复也是常见情况，这时按第 4 节的规则处理。
On the public route, having the patch merged is welcome and is not a criterion: the maintainer decides when, not you. A week of silence after sending is common too; concept 4 says what to do then.

选公开路线之前，先知道三件事：

Before you choose the public route, know three things:

  • 你的真实姓名和邮箱会随邮件永久留在 lore.kernel.org 的公开存档里，之后无法删除。内核不接受匿名贡献，submitting-patches.rst 的原话是 “using a known identity (sorry, no anonymous contributions.)”。
  • Your real name and e-mail address stay with the mail in the public archive on lore.kernel.org for good and cannot be deleted later. The kernel takes no anonymous contributions; submitting-patches.rst says “using a known identity (sorry, no anonymous contributions.)”.
  • 邮件会到达真实的维护者和列表的所有订阅者，占用他们的时间。回复可能很直接，也可能一个星期都没有回复。
  • The mail reaches real maintainers and every subscriber of the list, and takes their time. A reply may be blunt, and there may be none for a week.
  • 邮件发出后无法撤回。所以先把本地路线的检查全部做完，再决定发不发。
  • A mail that has been sent cannot be recalled. So finish every check of the local route first, then decide whether to send.

背景知识

Background

五个概念，每个只讲到够你完成任务为止。出处都是内核自带的文档，按 v7.3.1 核对。

Five concepts, each taken only as far as the task needs. The sources are the kernel’s own documents, checked at v7.3.1.

1. 补丁怎样走到主线

1. How a patch reaches mainline

内核没有「提交 pull request」这回事。补丁以纯文本邮件发到子系统的邮件列表，任何订阅者都能评审。维护者认可后把它收进自己的 git 树；各个子系统的树每天被合成一棵 linux-next，它是「下一个合并窗口结束后主线大致的样子」的快照。

The kernel has no “open a pull request”. A patch goes as a plain-text e-mail to the subsystem’s mailing list, where any subscriber may review it. Once the maintainer is satisfied it goes into the maintainer’s own git tree; the subsystem trees are merged every day into linux-next, a snapshot of what mainline is expected to look like after the next merge window closes.

每个开发周期开头有一个大约两周的合并窗口，维护者在这段时间把积累的改动交给 Linus；窗口关闭时发布 -rc1，之后的六到十周只收修复，直到正式版发布。所以你的补丁从被收下到出现在正式版里，隔两三个月是正常的。

Each development cycle opens with a merge window of about two weeks, in which maintainers hand their accumulated changes to Linus; -rc1 marks its close, and for the next six to ten weeks only fixes go in, until the release. Two or three months between your patch being applied and its appearing in a release is therefore normal.

延伸阅读：Documentation/process/2.Process.rst，「The big picture」和「How patches get into the Kernel」两节。

Further reading: Documentation/process/2.Process.rst, the sections “The big picture” and “How patches get into the Kernel”.

2. 一封补丁邮件的结构

2. The anatomy of a patch e-mail

git format-patch 把一个提交变成一封邮件。下面是热身里实际生成的一封（v2），从上到下有五部分：

git format-patch turns one commit into one e-mail. Below is one that the warm-up actually produced (a v2); it has five parts from top to bottom:

From: Ada Learner <ada@example.org>
Subject: [PATCH v2] staging: nvec: describe the gpiod member of struct
 nvec_chip

kernel-doc reports that the comment above struct nvec_chip describes a
member named gpio, while the member is called gpiod:

  nvec.h:161 struct member 'gpiod' not described in 'nvec_chip'
  nvec.h:161 Excess struct member 'gpio' description in 'nvec_chip'

The description also points at struct nvec_platform_data, which no
longer exists in the tree. Rename the entry and say what the member is:
the descriptor of the "request" GPIO that tegra_nvec_probe() obtains
and nvec_gpio_set_value() drives.

Signed-off-by: Ada Learner <ada@example.org>
---

Notes:
    v2: describe the member instead of pointing at struct nvec_platform_data,
        which is gone; shorten the quoted warnings to fit 75 columns.
    v1: (link to v1 on lore.kernel.org goes here)

 drivers/staging/nvec/nvec.h | 2 +-
 1 file changed, 1 insertion(+), 1 deletion(-)

diff --git a/drivers/staging/nvec/nvec.h b/drivers/staging/nvec/nvec.h
...
  • 主题行：方括号里是给工具看的标记（PATCH、版本号 v2），后面是「子系统前缀: 摘要」。摘要不超过 70–75 个字符，要同时说出改了什么和为什么值得改。
  • Subject: the square brackets hold tags for tools (PATCH, the version v2), then “subsystem prefix: summary”. The summary is at most 70–75 characters and says both what changes and why it is worth changing.
  • 正文：先讲问题（这里直接贴了工具的输出），再讲你做了什么。用祈使语气：写「Rename the entry」，不写「This patch renames」或「I renamed」。每行不超过 75 列。
  • Body: the problem first (here the tool output is quoted), then what you do about it. Imperative mood: “Rename the entry”, not “This patch renames” or “I renamed”. Lines stay within 75 columns.
  • 标签：Signed-off-by: 是你对「开发者原创声明」（DCO）的签署，表示你有权以内核的许可证提交这段改动，必须是真实姓名。Reviewed-by:、Acked-by:、Tested-by: 是别人给你的，不能自己加。修的是某个提交引入的 bug 时，加 Fixes:，写该提交哈希的至少前 12 位和它的摘要。
  • Tags: Signed-off-by: is your signature under the Developer’s Certificate of Origin, stating that you have the right to submit the change under the kernel’s licence; it must be your real name. Reviewed-by:, Acked-by: and Tested-by: are given to you by others and are never added by yourself. When you fix a bug that a particular commit introduced, add Fixes: with at least the first 12 characters of that commit’s hash and its summary.
  • --- 之后：这条线以下、diff 以上的内容不会进入 git 历史。每一版与上一版的差别写在这里。
  • After the ---: what sits below this line and above the diff does not enter the git history. The differences between versions go here.
  • diff：内联在邮件正文里，不能是附件，这样评审者才能在你的代码行之间逐行回复。
  • The diff: inline in the body, never an attachment, so that reviewers can reply between your lines of code.

延伸阅读：Documentation/process/submitting-patches.rst，「The canonical patch format」和「Sign your work - the Developer’s Certificate of Origin」两节。

3. 工具给的是线索，不是结论

3. Tools give leads, not verdicts

你会用到四个随源码发布的工具：scripts/checkpatch.pl 检查风格和补丁格式；scripts/get_maintainer.pl 根据 MAINTAINERS 文件算出一个补丁该发给谁；scripts/kernel-doc 检查函数和结构体上方的文档注释是否和代码对得上；编译时加 W=1 会打开更多编译器警告。

You will use four tools that ship with the source: scripts/checkpatch.pl checks style and patch format; scripts/get_maintainer.pl works out from the MAINTAINERS file who a patch goes to; scripts/kernel-doc checks that the documentation comments above functions and structures match the code; building with W=1 turns on more compiler warnings.

它们都不理解代码的意图。checkpatch.pl 是逐行做模式匹配的脚本，它的建议可能让代码编译不过（热身 A 会让你亲眼看到一次）；kernel-doc 只比较名字，名字对上了、描述却早已过时，它也不会报（热身 B）。所以规则只有一条：工具指出位置，你自己读代码判断，然后编译验证。新手最常见的被拒理由，就是照着工具的输出改了一行没编译过的代码。

None of them understands what the code means. checkpatch.pl is a script that matches patterns line by line, and its suggestion can leave code that does not compile (warm-up A lets you see that happen); kernel-doc compares names only, and stays silent when the name matches while the description went stale long ago (warm-up B). So there is one rule: the tool points at a place, you read the code and decide, and then you build it. The most common reason a newcomer’s patch is rejected is a line changed on a tool’s say-so and never compiled.

4. 评审是一场公开的书面对话

4. Review is a public conversation in writing

评审意见以回信的形式出现，对方会引用你的补丁，在相关的代码行下面写意见。你的回复也要这样写：保留与你的回答有关的引用，把回答写在它下面，删掉其余部分。不要把回复整段写在邮件顶部（top-posting），不要发 HTML 邮件。

Review arrives as replies that quote your patch and comment under the relevant lines. Your answers take the same form: keep the quote your answer refers to, write the answer under it, trim the rest. Do not put the whole reply at the top of the mail (top-posting), and do not send HTML.

  • 每条意见都要回应。接受就说会在下一版改；不同意就说明技术理由。无视评审者的人，下一次也会被无视。
  • Every comment gets an answer. If you accept it, say the next version will change; if you disagree, give the technical reason. People who ignore reviewers are ignored in turn.
  • 改完后发新版本：[PATCH v2] 是一封新邮件、一个新讨论串，正文完整，--- 下面写清与 v1 的差别。评审过上一版的人要在收件人里。
  • After changing the patch, post a new version: [PATCH v2] is a new mail and a new thread with the complete description, and the differences from v1 go below the ---. People who reviewed the previous version are among the recipients.
  • 没有回复时，至少等一个星期再询问或重发，合并窗口期间要更久。原样重发时在主题里加 RESEND；改过内容的不算重发，要升版本号。
  • With no reply, wait at least a week before asking or resending, longer during a merge window. An unchanged resend carries RESEND in the subject; a changed patch is not a resend and gets a new version number.
  • 评审者偶尔语气生硬。回复时保持礼貌，只谈技术问题，并感谢对方花的时间。
  • Reviewers are sometimes curt. Answer politely, stay on the technical point, and thank them for their time.

延伸阅读：Documentation/process/submitting-patches.rst，「Respond to review comments」「Use trimmed interleaved replies in email discussions」「Don’t get discouraged - or impatient」三节；Documentation/process/6.Followthrough.rst。

Further reading: Documentation/process/submitting-patches.rst, the sections “Respond to review comments”, “Use trimmed interleaved replies in email discussions” and “Don’t get discouraged - or impatient”; Documentation/process/6.Followthrough.rst.

5. 如果你用了 AI 工具

5. If an AI tool helped you

内核对此有成文的规则，写在 Documentation/process/coding-assistants.rst 和 generated-content.rst 里。v7.3.1 的要点有四条：

The kernel has written rules for this, in Documentation/process/coding-assistants.rst and generated-content.rst. At v7.3.1 they come to four points:

  • Signed-off-by: 只能由人来加。工具不能替你签，你签了就要对整个补丁负全责。
  • Only a human adds Signed-off-by:. A tool cannot sign for you, and once you sign you carry full responsibility for the whole patch.
  • 补丁里有相当一部分内容出自工具时要公开说明：用了什么工具，哪些部分受影响，怎样测试的。v7.3.1 的文件还要求加一行 Assisted-by: 标签。
  • When a meaningful part of the patch came from a tool, say so openly: which tool, which parts, how it was tested. The file at v7.3.1 also asks for an Assisted-by: tag.
  • 你必须理解并能为你提交的每一行辩护。做不到，就不要提交；维护者有权不经详细评审就拒绝这样的补丁。
  • You must understand and be able to defend every line you submit. If you cannot, do not submit it; maintainers are entitled to reject such a patch without detailed review.
  • 助手绝不能自己发送任何东西。发信的人永远是你。
  • The assistant must never send anything itself. The sender is always you.
这是一条还在变化的规则：docs.kernel.org 上 7.4.0-rc3 的同一页（2026-11-21 读取）已经没有「Attribution」一节，也不再提 Assisted-by:。发补丁之前，读你所基于的那棵树里的这两个文件。
This rule is still moving: the same page for 7.4.0-rc3 on docs.kernel.org (read 2026-11-21) no longer has the “Attribution” section and does not mention Assisted-by:. Before you send, read these two files in the tree your patch is based on.

Warm-up

热身 A · 照着 checkpatch 改，然后编译

Warm-up A · Do what checkpatch says, then build

  1. 在内核源码树里开一个练习分支，让 checkpatch.pl 检查一个 staging 驱动的整个文件（-f 表示检查文件而不是补丁）：In the kernel tree, start a practice branch and let checkpatch.pl check a whole file of a staging driver (-f means a file, not a patch): 
    git switch -c practice/sm750 v7.3.1
./scripts/checkpatch.pl -f drivers/staging/sm750fb/sm750.c
    WARNING: static const char * array should probably be static const char * const
#15: FILE: drivers/staging/sm750fb/sm750.c:15:
+static const char *g_fbmode[] = {NULL, NULL};

total: 0 errors, 1 warnings, 0 checks, 1147 lines checked
    如果先打印出一段以 ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'ply' 结尾的 Traceback，那是 checkpatch.pl 调用的许可证检查脚本缺一个 Python 模块，装上发行版的 ply 包即可（Debian 和 Ubuntu 上是 python3-ply）。If a Traceback ending in ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'ply' is printed first, the licence checker that checkpatch.pl calls is missing a Python module; install your distribution’s ply package (python3-ply on Debian and Ubuntu).
  2. 照它说的改，草草提交，生成补丁，再检查这个补丁：Do as it says, commit carelessly, generate the patch and check the patch: 
    sed -i 's/^static const char \*g_fbmode\[\] = /static const char * const g_fbmode[] = /' \

正式任务

The assignment

现在换成你自己找的缺陷。下面只有目标和约束，没有逐步命令；卡住时再展开提示。前五步两条路线都一样，第六步选一条路线收尾。

Now with a defect you find yourself. What follows is goals and constraints, with no step-by-step commands; open a hint when you are stuck. The first five steps are the same on both routes; step six finishes by one of them.

  1. 选一棵当前的树。完整克隆 linux-next，或者你打算改的子系统在 MAINTAINERS 里 T: 行给出的树。staging 驱动的树是 gregkh/staging.git，列表是 linux-staging@lists.linux.dev。
  2. Pick a current tree. Make a full clone of linux-next, or of the tree that the T: line in MAINTAINERS gives for the subsystem you mean to change. For staging drivers the tree is gregkh/staging.git and the list is linux-staging@lists.linux.dev.
  3. 找一处真实的小缺陷。它必须在这棵树的最新提交上仍然存在，而且你能用一两句话说清它为什么是缺陷。
  4. Find a small real defect. It must still be present at the tip of that tree, and you must be able to say in a sentence or two why it is a defect.
  5. 确认没有人已经在修。在 lore.kernel.org 上搜索文件名和关键字。同一处已经有人发过补丁时，换一个目标。
  6. Make sure nobody is already fixing it. Search lore.kernel.org for the file name and keywords. If a patch for the same spot has been posted, pick another target.
  7. 写补丁并验证。一个补丁只做一件事。编译改到的文件；改动影响运行时行为时，在虚拟机里实际跑一次。
  8. Write the patch and verify it. One patch does one thing. Build the files you touched; if the change affects run-time behaviour, run it in the VM.
  9. 先发给自己。用 get_maintainer.pl 算出收件人并记下来，用 git send-email --dry-run 确认将要发出的内容，然后只发给自己，在邮箱里检查格式。
  10. Send it to yourself first. Work out the recipients with get_maintainer.pl and write them down, confirm with git send-email --dry-run what would go out, then mail it to yourself alone and check the format in your mailbox.
  11. 选一条路线收尾。本地路线：至少隔一天，像评审别人的补丁那样对照验收标准重读它，把发现写下来，需要时做 v2。公开路线（由学习者完成）：按 get_maintainer.pl 的输出发给维护者和列表，在 lore 上找到你的讨论串并保存链接，回复评审，需要时发 v2。
  12. Finish by one route. Local route: at least a day later, reread the patch against the acceptance criteria as you would review someone else’s, write down what you find, and make a v2 when needed. Public route (done by the learner): send it to the maintainers and lists that get_maintainer.pl prints, find your thread on lore and keep the link, answer the review, and post a v2 when needed.
提示：去哪里找第一个补丁Hint: where to look for a first patch

按「越容易验证越靠前」排列。每一类找到的都只是候选，都要读代码确认：

Ordered from easiest to verify. Whatever each one turns up is only a candidate; read the code to confirm:

  • kernel-doc 警告：./scripts/kernel-doc -none <文件>。注释与代码对不上是真实缺陷，修复容易验证（再跑一次工具，警告消失）。热身 B 里 nvec.h 第 78 行那三条枚举值的警告就是这一类。
  • kernel-doc warnings: ./scripts/kernel-doc -none <file>. A comment that does not match the code is a real defect and the fix is easy to verify (run the tool again, the warning is gone). The three enum warnings at line 78 of nvec.h in warm-up B are of this kind.
  • 文档里的错误：Documentation/ 下过时的路径、写错的命令、失效的引用。你读文档做实验时碰到的每个错，都可能是一个补丁。
  • Errors in documentation: stale paths, wrong commands and dead references under Documentation/. Every mistake you hit while following a document in a lab may be a patch.
  • staging 驱动：每个驱动目录下的 TODO 文件列出了维护者希望有人做的事；checkpatch.pl -f 给出风格线索。staging 是明确欢迎新手清理的地方。
  • Staging drivers: the TODO file in each driver directory lists what the maintainers want done; checkpatch.pl -f gives style leads. Staging is the place where cleanups from newcomers are explicitly welcome.
  • W=1 编译警告：make W=1 <目录>/，例如未使用的变量、缺少原型的函数。
  • W=1 build warnings: make W=1 <directory>/, for example unused variables and functions without a prototype.

不要发的：只改空白或只重排代码的补丁（staging 之外很少被接受）；codespell 报的「拼写错误」里有大量是变量名（在 v7.3.1 的 staging 上跑一遍，报出来的几乎全是 te、filp、parm 这样的标识符）；以及任何你没编译过的改动。

Do not send: patches that only change whitespace or reorder code (rarely accepted outside staging); the “spelling mistakes” codespell reports, a large share of which are variable names (run it over staging at v7.3.1 and nearly everything it lists is an identifier such as te, filp or parm); and any change you have not built.

提示：发信前的最后检查Hint: the last check before sending
  • 补丁能干净地打在目标树的最新提交上：git fetch 之后 git rebase，重新编译。
  • The patch applies cleanly to the tip of the target tree: git fetch, git rebase, build again.
  • checkpatch.pl 检查的是补丁文件，不是源文件。
  • checkpatch.pl is run on the patch file, not on the source file.
  • 提交信息里出现的每个函数名、文件名、行号都查过。
  • Every function name, file name and line number in the commit message has been checked.
  • 先发给自己，在邮箱里看原始邮件：纯文本，Tab 没有变成空格，没有被自动换行。
  • Sent to yourself first and inspected as raw mail: plain text, tabs not turned into spaces, no automatic wrapping.
  • git format-patch --base=auto 会在补丁末尾记下它所基于的提交，评审者和自动测试能据此还原你的树。
  • git format-patch --base=auto records at the end of the patch which commit it is based on, so reviewers and test robots can reconstruct your tree.
提示：公开路线，收到评审之后Hint: the public route, after the review arrives
  • 一天之内回复，哪怕只是「收到，下一版改」。
  • Answer within a day, even if only to say “thanks, will fix in the next version”.
  • 不要在同一天连发 v2、v3。攒齐所有意见，改完，重新走一遍「编译、checkpatch、发给自己」再发。
  • Do not fire off v2 and v3 on the same day. Collect all the comments, make the changes, go through “build, checkpatch, send to yourself” again, then send.
  • 别人给了 Reviewed-by: 而你之后没有改动那部分内容时，把这一行加进下一版的提交信息。
  • When someone gave a Reviewed-by: and you have not changed that part since, add the line to the commit message of the next version.
  • 想少记一些手工步骤，可以了解 b4：它的文档说 b4 prep 负责把补丁系列准备好，b4 send 负责发送（--reflect 只发给自己，--dry-run 不发信），b4 trailers 负责收回别人给的评审标签。本页没有运行 b4。

自测

Self-check

1. checkpatch.pl -f 建议给一个数组加 const。你改了，checkpatch.pl 不再报警告。发出去之前还缺哪一步？

1. checkpatch.pl -f suggests adding const to an array. You make the change and checkpatch.pl is silent. What is still missing before you send?

工具只做模式匹配。热身 A 里同样的建议让编译直接失败，因为数组元素在运行时被赋值。Reviewed-by: 只能由别人给；主题行应该描述改动，checkpatch 自己就会对「主题写的是工具」报警告。The tool only matches patterns. In warm-up A the same suggestion broke the build, because the array elements are assigned at run time. Reviewed-by: comes only from others; the subject describes the change, and checkpatch itself warns when the subject names the tool.

2. 你发 [PATCH v2] 时，「与 v1 相比改了什么」写在哪里？

2. When you post [PATCH v2], where does “what changed since v1” go?

--- 以下的内容不进 git 历史，版本间的差别只对这一轮评审有用，所以放在那里。写进正文会把它永久留在历史里；不写则让评审者重读整个补丁，而且有些评审者根本没收到上一版。What is below the --- stays out of the git history, and the differences between versions matter only for this round of review, so they belong there. Putting them in the body would keep them in history for ever; leaving them out makes reviewers reread the whole patch, and some reviewers never received the previous version.

3. 补丁发出去四天了，没有任何回复。按内核文档，现在该怎么做？

3. Four days after sending, there has been no reply at all. According to the kernel documentation, what do you do now?

文档要求至少等一个星期，合并窗口期间更久。极少有补丁直接经过 Linus，文档明确说应尽量避免给他发信。没有实质改动却升版本号会误导评审者，RESEND 才是「内容没变」的标记。The documentation asks for at least a week, longer during a merge window. Very few patches go through Linus directly and the documentation says to avoid mailing him. Raising the version number without a real change misleads reviewers; RESEND is the mark for “nothing changed”.

4. 你的补丁里有一个函数是 AI 助手写的。按 v7.3.1 的内核文档，哪种做法是对的？

4. One function in your patch was written by an AI assistant. According to the kernel documentation at v7.3.1, which is right?

只有人能签署 DCO，工具不能加 Signed-off-by:，也绝不能自己发信。出自工具的内容要公开说明，v7.3.1 的文件给出的方式是 Assisted-by:。你要能为每一行辩护，否则维护者可以不经详细评审就拒绝。Only a human can sign the DCO; a tool adds no Signed-off-by: and never sends anything itself. Content that came from a tool is disclosed, and the file at v7.3.1 gives Assisted-by: as the way. You must be able to defend every line, or maintainers may reject the patch without detailed review.
